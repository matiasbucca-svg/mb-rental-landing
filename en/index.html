Your questions answered

Frequently asked questions

01Who is this service for?

Companies that manage short-term rentals and need help with specific operational tasks, either on an ongoing basis or during busier periods.

02Which tasks can my company hand off?

You can hand off bookings and guest communication, issue coordination, help with listings and reviews, or property onboarding and sales follow-up. We define the scope around your team’s priorities.

03Can you work with my company’s tools and suppliers?

Yes. I work within your platforms, your communication channels and the suppliers you already use. Before we start, we agree on access and the procedure for each task.

04Can I book extra support for one season or a holiday period?

Yes, subject to availability. We can set up a one-off arrangement for specific dates, tasks and hours.

05What hours does the service cover?

Coverage is agreed before we start. We define the time slots, the support channels and who urgent reports or out-of-hours issues should be passed on to.

06How can my team check on open issues?

We agree where reports are logged, how to check their status and when to update your team. That way it is easy to see what is still pending and what needs a decision from your side.

07Which areas do you cover?

I work remotely with companies all over Spain. In and around Seville, I can also handle occasional on-site tasks at the properties, arranged in advance.

08How is pricing worked out?

Each proposal is based on the tasks involved, the number of properties, the level of activity and the hours of coverage. Before we begin, we agree on the scope and terms of the service.
